<p class="home-intro">I am a researcher at <a href="https://www.fbk.eu">Fondazione Bruno Kessler</a> in Trento, Italy, in the Planning, Scheduling, and Optimization unit of the Digital Industry Center. I work on <b>formal methods for artificial intelligence</b>: building models and algorithms that give guarantees on how plans, processes, and AI systems behave, particularly under uncertainty.</p>

<div class="home-label">Research interests</div>
<div class="entry__chips">
  <span class="chip">Formal methods for AI</span>
  <span class="chip">Neuro-symbolic AI</span>
  <span class="chip">Temporal networks</span>
  <span class="chip">Scheduling under uncertainty</span>
  <span class="chip">Business process control, verification, and repair</span>
</div>
