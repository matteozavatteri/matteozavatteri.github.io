<p class="home-intro">I am a researcher at <a href="https://www.fbk.eu">Fondazione Bruno Kessler</a> in Trento, Italy, in the Planning, Scheduling, and Optimization unit of the Digital Industry Center. I work on <b>formal methods for artificial intelligence</b>: building models and algorithms that give guarantees on how plans, processes, and AI systems behave, particularly under uncertainty.</p>

<div class="home-label">Research interests</div>
<div class="entry__chips">
  <span class="chip">Formal methods for AI</span>
  <span class="chip">Neuro-symbolic AI</span>
  <span class="chip">Temporal networks</span>
  <span class="chip">Scheduling under uncertainty</span>
  <span class="chip">Business process control, verification, and repair</span>
</div>

<div class="home-label">Research directions</div>
<div class="research-grid">

  <div class="research-card">
    <div class="research-card__title">Temporal networks</div>
    <p>Plans made of events constrained in time, with parts the planner cannot control. I study whether a plan can always be executed whatever the uncontrollable part does, and how to synthesize the strategy that executes it.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="research-card">
    <div class="research-card__title">Neuro-symbolic AI</div>
    <p>Using formal methods to bring background knowledge into AI systems, and to automatically synthesize neural networks that are certified to satisfy given properties while keeping their accuracy.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="research-card">
    <div class="research-card__title">Business processes</div>
    <p>Formal models of business processes and algorithms to verify them, control their execution under uncertainty, and repair them with minimal changes when a property is violated.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="research-card">
    <div class="research-card__title">Digital twins</div>
    <p>Combining formal methods and AI to build data-driven models of complex systems, so that problems can be solved on the digital twin before moving to the real system.</p>
  </div>

</div>
