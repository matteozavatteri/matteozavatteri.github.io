  <div class="entry__years">Jan 2014 - May 2018</div>
  <div>
    <span class="entry__role">Ph.D. student</span>
    <div class="entry__name">Ph.D. in Computer Science (29th cycle)</div>
    <div class="entry__alt">Thesis: <a href="https://hdl.handle.net/11562/979769">Temporal and Resource Controllability of Workflows Under Uncertainty</a></div>
    <div class="entry__meta">
      <span>Department of Computer Science, University of Verona, Italy</span>
      <span><b>Advisor:</b> Prof. Luca Viganò. <b>Co-advisor:</b> Prof. Carlo Combi</span>
      <span>Full Italian scholarship</span>
    </div>
    <p>In my Ph.D. thesis I addressed the design, validation, and synthesis of execution strategies for workflow/business processes under uncertainty. I gave particular emphasis to the temporal and resource aspects, considered both in isolation or simultaneously. I defined new temporal network models that address several kinds of uncertainty (temporal, conditional, etc) and I also developed several algorithms for synthesizing execution strategies with respect to the classic problems of weak, strong, and dynamic controllability. I applied such models in the context of workflow/business processes by providing a few encodings from a few fragments of the Business Process Modeling Notation (BPMN) into temporal networks.</p>
